How To Use

Prepare Your Scalp

Ensure your hair and scalp are dry and free from styling products.

Power On

Press the button to turn it on — vibration starts automatically.

Select Mode

Pick the LED mode based on your hair goals

Start Brushing

Glide the brush slowly over the area, focusing on thinning spots.

Switch Lights

Use each mode 1-2 minutes. You can switch colors anytime.

Make It a Ritual

Use at least 3-4 times weekly for 3-10 minutes.
